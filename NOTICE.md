## Origin

- **begosik** made the original Red Alert 3 eight-player support. The `.8pv1` UI pages come from the conversion of that work in [RA3BattleNet/RA3_8P_FULL_EN](https://github.com/RA3BattleNet/RA3_8P_FULL_EN) (release v6), recorded in `docs/aptui/provenance.json`.
- **RA3BattleNet** integrated it and maintains the source repo, [RA3BattleNet/Content](https://github.com/RA3BattleNet/Content). Files here were imported from it with their git history, so the original authors (Lanyi, lanyizi, RC and 白学森) are kept on every commit.
- The maps were made for RA3BattleNet by their respective authors and are shipped under their original names.

## Changes made for Tacitus

Only the eight-player pages and maps were kept. The map registration was split into new files, `tools/package.py` and the `priority` folder were added, and `tools/aptui/check_release.py` was trimmed. Everything else is as imported.
